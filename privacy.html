Privacy Policy

MT Research Uploader · last updated 9 November 2026

MT Research Uploader is an internal tool used only by MT Research to upload its own short videos to its own YouTube channel, @mtresearchxyz. It is not offered to the public and no one outside MT Research signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and post a comment under them, on the channel owner's own channel only. It reads the channel's name to confirm it is connected to the right channel. It does not read or collect information about other YouTube users or viewers.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and a list of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or through the Google security settings page. Deleting the token file on the computer removes the stored data.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
